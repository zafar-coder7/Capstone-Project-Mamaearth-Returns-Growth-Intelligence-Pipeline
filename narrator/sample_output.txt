Situation
Total raw revenue reported was ₹99,860.20. After a rigorous data cleansing process, we identified a system anomaly leading to double-submitted orders. Removing these duplicates created a reconciliation delta of ₹2,501.90, resulting in a verified, cleaned total revenue of ₹97,358.30. Furthermore, removing anomalous bulk orders (quantity outliers) shifted our understanding of demand, revealing that March was our true peak month driving ₹20,318.90 in revenue, correcting the inflated perspective that January was leading.

Complication
The core threat to our operating margin lies in return rates, which are highly skewed by payment method. Analysis proves that Cash on Delivery (COD) carries a massive return rate of 44.4%, drastically outperforming CARD (14.7%) and UPI (18.9%). The complication compounds when geolocated: our highest-risk segment is Tier-2 cities utilizing COD, which spikes to a staggering 54.5% return rate.

Resolution
To protect margins, leadership must take immediate action on Tier-2 COD dependencies. We recommend implementing strict verification steps or nominal convenience fees for COD orders targeting Tier-2 cities to suppress the 54.5% return leakage. Marketing should pivot to heavily incentivize prepaid methods like UPI and CARD, focusing peak promotion drives to mimic the genuine organic success seen in March (₹20,318.90).
